## FAQ (Contact / Home)

1. **What treatments can be offered?**  
   Depending on your licensed scope and team, services may include prevention, restorative care, cosmetic options, orthodontics, and implants. Exact availability is clinic-specific.

2. **How do new patients start?**  
   Book an initial consultation for history, examination, and discussion of options without pressure to proceed the same day.

3. **How does planning work?**  
   After assessment and indicated imaging, you receive a written plan covering proposed steps, alternatives, approximate timelines, and fees before treatment begins.

4. **How long does treatment take?**  
   Timelines vary by clinical findings. Whitening or simple restorations may be shorter; implants and orthodontics are phased and assessment-dependent.

5. **Do you accept insurance?**  
   `[Buyer configures]` Many Dubai clinics support direct billing with selected insurers; always verify benefits for dental cover.
