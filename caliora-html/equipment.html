Equipment

Technology in service of judgement

The tools we use for diagnosis and treatment — and why each one earns its place in the clinic.

3D CBCT imaging

Cone-beam CT provides three-dimensional views of teeth and bone, used when clinically justified for implant planning and complex root canal cases.

Intraoral scanning

Digital scans replace conventional impression trays for most cases, improving comfort and accuracy for aligners, crowns and veneers.

Operating microscope

High magnification helps clinicians see fine detail during root canal treatment and precision restorative work.

Sterilisation suite

Dedicated decontamination room with validated autoclave cycles and full instrument tracking, in line with DHA infection-control requirements.
